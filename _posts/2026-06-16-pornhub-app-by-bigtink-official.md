## Why the Rebrand Matters for USA Users

Google's search results, app store snippets, and third-party app indexes all cache descriptions and metadata on their own schedules. As of mid-2026, you can still run a Google search for the package id and get back a result where the **og:description** meta tag reads something close to "Crimzle Yumy: Candy Creator" even though the live Play Store page has been showing the Pornhub branding for months.

That's not a sign that something's wrong with the app. It's a stale cache. Google re-crawls Play Store pages on its own timeline, and the og:description field is one of the slowest pieces of metadata to update because it's pulled into so many downstream search products and link previews. Until that refresh happens, US users who land on the app through a Google search may see a snippet that doesn't match what the Play Store actually shows when they tap through.

The thing to anchor on is the live Play Store page itself. If the developer line reads Bigtink and the package id is `com.crimzleyumy.icrimzuyumy`, you're on the official listing regardless of what any cached snippet says around it.
