## Common Install Errors and Fixes

| Error | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| App not available in your country | Play Store region is not US | Settings → Country and profiles → switch to United States, wait 24 hrs |
| Download stuck at 0% | Stale Play Store cache | Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache, retry |
| Pending install | Another download is queued | Open Play Store → Manage apps → cancel other pending items |
| Insufficient storage | Under 20 MB free | Delete unused apps or clear cache on heavy apps (Chrome, Photos) |
| Play Protect warning | First-time install of a new adult app on your device | Tap **Install anyway** — the app is signed by Bigtink and verified on Play |
| Error code DF-DLA-15 | Corrupted Play Services data | Settings → Apps → Google Play Services → Storage → Clear cache, reboot |
| Authentication required | Google account session expired | Sign out of Play Store, sign back in |

If none of the above resolve it, reboot the phone and retry. That fixes the long tail of one-off Play Services glitches.
