# How to Install the Pornhub App on Android in 2026 (Step by Step)

This is a step-by-step install walkthrough for **Crimzle Yumy** — the only **pornhub app** that ships through Google Play with a verified listing. The whole process takes about two minutes, no APK sideloading, no third-party stores, no "enable unknown sources" toggle.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- An Android phone or tablet running **Android 7.0 or newer**
- A Google account (the same one you use for any other Play Store app)
- About **50 MB of free storage**
- A stable internet connection for streaming (Wi-Fi recommended for HD)

The app itself is free. There is no paid tier, no subscription, and no "premium unlock" prompt.

## Step 1: Open the Google Play Store

Tap the Play Store icon on your home screen or app drawer. If you don't have the Play Store installed, your device is running a non-Google build of Android and this walkthrough won't apply.

## Step 2: Search for "pornhub app" or "Crimzle Yumy"

In the Play Store search bar, type either:

- **Crimzle Yumy** — the brand name, returns the app as the top result
- **pornhub app** — returns the app plus a list of unofficial alternatives (avoid those)

Tap the listing titled **Crimzle Yumy** by Bigtink.

## Step 3: Tap Install

The Install button is in the top-right of the listing. Tap it once. The download takes 10-30 seconds on a typical connection.

The Play Store will show a permissions preview before the install completes. Read this carefully — the only permissions listed should be:

- Internet access
- Network state
- Optional storage access (for offline downloads)

If the listing asks for anything else (contacts, location, microphone, etc.), back out — that's not the right app.

## Step 4: Tap Open

Once the install finishes, the Install button changes to Open. Tap it to launch Crimzle Yumy for the first time.

## Step 5: Accept the content notice

The first launch shows a brief content notice (age confirmation, terms of service). Tap through it. There is **no account creation step** — you can use the app anonymously. Skip anything that asks you to register.

## Step 6: Browse by category or search

The home screen is divided into a few sections:

- **Trending** — what's popular right now
- **Categories** — full category list, sorted by typical adult-site taxonomy
- **Search bar** (top) — search by performer, tag, or category
- **Watch history** — if you've used the app before, your history is here

Tap any thumbnail to start playback. The first video takes 2-3 seconds to buffer depending on your connection; subsequent videos start almost instantly.

## Step 7: Set up offline mode (optional)

If you want to save videos for offline viewing:

1. Tap the **download** icon on any video thumbnail
2. Choose a quality (lower = smaller file, higher = sharper picture)
3. Wait for the download to complete — there's a notification when it's done
4. Access downloaded videos from the **Downloads** tab in the side menu

Downloaded videos are stored in the app's private storage, so they don't show up in your regular gallery. If you want extra privacy, encrypt the device with a PIN — the standard Android disk encryption covers the app's private storage automatically.

## First-time tips

- **No account needed** — don't enter personal information anywhere. The app doesn't need it
- **VPN optional** — use one if you don't want your ISP to see the traffic
- **Avoid browser handoffs** — if the app ever tries to bounce you out to a browser to "verify" something or "claim a prize," back out. That's a phishing pattern
- **Updates are automatic** — the Play Store updates the app in the background, no action needed

## What if the install fails?

The most common reasons:

1. **Device too old** — needs Android 7.0+. Check under Settings → About phone → Android version
2. **Insufficient storage** — free up 100 MB and retry
3. **Play Store out of date** — open the Play Store, let it update itself, then retry
4. **Region restriction** — if the listing doesn't appear at all, the app may not be available in your country. Use a VPN and try a region where the app is published

## Uninstall

Standard Android uninstall: long-press the icon → App info → Uninstall. Nothing is left behind in shared storage, and there's no account to delete.
