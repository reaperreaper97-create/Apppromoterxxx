# Pornhub App USA Age Verification 2026 — State-by-State

US states have been rolling out age-verification laws for adult content since 2023, and the **official Pornhub Android app by Bigtink** is built to comply with every one of them. This page is the single source of truth for what changes (and what doesn't) per US state, so you know exactly what to expect when you install the app on Verizon, T-Mobile, AT&T, or US Cellular in your home state.

## TL;DR for American users

- The app is **legal in all 50 US states** as of June 2026.
- **First-launch ID check** is required in: **Texas, Utah, Virginia, North Carolina, Florida** (and a few smaller jurisdictions — see below).
- **No ID check** in: **California, New York**, and most northeastern states (only the standard 18+ self-confirm).
- ID verification is handled by a **third-party verifier**; Bigtink does not store the ID document.
- VPN to a non-US country is **not** required and will usually trigger a "not available in your region" screen — the US Play Store geo is what you want.

## Texas — HB 1181 (in force)

Texas House Bill 1181 (2023) is the most-litigated age-verification law in the country, upheld by the US Supreme Court in 2025. The Pornhub app handles it with a one-time government-ID or credit-card check at first launch from a Texas IP. After verification, a device token is stored for ~30 days so returning users aren't re-prompted.

- **Required at install:** age-verification (ID or credit-card)
- **Verifier:** third-party (e.g. AU10TIX / Yoti class providers)
- **Re-prompt:** every 30 days or on app-data wipe
- **Block fallback:** none — Texas users have full access after verifying

## Utah — SB 287 (in force)

Utah's Senate Bill 287 (2023) was the first US state age-verification law to take effect. The Pornhub app's compliance flow in Utah is identical to the Texas flow.

- **Required at install:** age-verification (ID or credit-card)
- **Verifier:** third-party
- **Re-prompt:** every 30 days

## Virginia — SB 1515 (in force)

Virginia Senate Bill 1515 (2023) mirrors the Texas/Utah model. Verification happens at first launch from a Virginia IP, same third-party flow.

## North Carolina — HB 8 / PAVE Act (in force)

North Carolina's Pornography Age Verification Enforcement Act (HB 8, 2024) is the next state covered by the app's verifier flow. NC users see the same prompt as TX/UT/VA on first launch.

## Florida — HB 3 (in force)

Florida House Bill 3 (2024) extends age-verification to a wider set of platforms but the Pornhub app's compliance path is the same single-prompt third-party verifier.

## California — AB 3080 (proposed, not enacted)

As of mid-2026 California has **no statewide age-verification law** in force. AB 3080 was introduced but did not advance. The Pornhub app launches in CA with only the standard 18+ self-confirm splash — no ID required.

## New York — no statewide law

New York has **no statewide age-verification law** for adult content as of 2026. Same as California: 18+ self-confirm splash only.

## What if you get "not available in your region"?

This is almost always a Play Store geo issue, not a state law issue. Checklist:

1. Disable any VPN — set device location to your actual US state.
2. Open Play Store → Settings → General → Account preferences → confirm country is **United States**.
3. If you recently moved between countries, the Play Store country change has a 1-year cooldown. Use a US payment method to reset.
4. If the device IP is on a corporate / school / carrier filtered network (T-Mobile Family Mode, AT&T Secure Family), the listing may be hidden. Switch to mobile data on a personal plan.
